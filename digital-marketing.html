PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT MODULE

Digital Marketing with Freelancing + AI

Start From Zero - Grow to Pro. Master content marketing with Ai, Meta Ads with AI, YouTube growth, Local & global freelancing platforms, Active & Passive income with Career guideline, Professional productivity-boosting with AI tools.

Program Overview

Are you a student, job holder, or homemaker looking for a proven roadmap to build a successful online career? This comprehensive 3-month Digital Marketing + Freelancing + AI course is designed to take you from absolute beginner to a professional digital marketer. You'll master social media marketing, creative content creation, advanced advertising campaigns, global freelancing on Fiverr & Upwork, and leverage cutting-edge AI tools like ChatGPT for high productivity.

Course Curriculum

  • Essential soft skills and hard skills required to succeed.
  • Traditional vs. Digital Marketing: Key differences and future trends.
  • Industry landscape: Why digital marketing is vital for modern careers.

  • Core digital marketing theories and strategies (A to Z).
  • Audience segmentation, persona creation, and customer journeys.
  • Goal setting, KPI tracking, and ROI calculations for digital campaigns.

  • Defining content marketing and understanding its diverse formats.
  • Copywriting essentials: crafting attention-grabbing headlines and body copy.
  • Creating content calendars and establishing a consistent brand voice.

  • Canva basics: Interface, custom dimensioning, and design alignment.
  • Creating social media graphics, posts, and branding materials from templates.
  • Leveraging Canva AI image generators and advanced AI creative suites.
  • Infographic design, color theory, typography, and professional GIF creation.

  • How to design and edit engaging promotional videos in Canva step-by-step.
  • Adding transitions, background audio tracks, video effects, and overlays.
  • Creating YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and TikTok ad videos.

  • Channel creation, verification, branding, and advanced layouts setup.
  • YouTube Video SEO: Optimizing titles, descriptions, playlists, and tags.
  • Growth tools: Mastering VidIQ and TubeBuddy extensions for analytics.
  • Demystifying the YouTube algorithm for 2026 growth and viral potential.

  • Setting up and managing professional Facebook & Instagram pages.
  • Page boosting basics vs. Advanced Meta Ads Manager campaigns.
  • Campaign structures: Ad Sets, targeting options, Custom & Lookalike Audiences.
  • Designing compelling ad creatives, writing copy, and configuring pixel tracking.
  • Setting up dual-currency cards, payment methods, and daily budget controls.

  • Setting up optimized freelancer profiles on Fiverr & Upwork.
  • Writing winning proposals, gig titles, tags, and pricing descriptions.
  • Mastering ChatGPT prompts to write captions, blog posts, and scripts in seconds.
  • Strategies for recurring passive income streams and local client acquisition.
