EXPERT LEADERSHIP

About STAR FAIR Advisors & Directors

The leadership team of STAR FAIR Fashion & Cultural Training Institute consists of experienced professionals from the fields of fashion, media, education, culture, business, and creative arts. Together, they provide strategic guidance, ensure organizational excellence, and guide the institute in developing future talents who can compete confidently on both national and international platforms.
